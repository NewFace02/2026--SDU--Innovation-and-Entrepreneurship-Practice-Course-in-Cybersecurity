Notes:
  GIFT is implemented in a separate file. Segment timing shows the scalar
  P-layer is much slower than the scalar S-box layer, so the real full-round
  hotspot is the bit permutation layer. The AVX2 vpshufb S-box kernel is fast,
  but it does not solve the P-layer bottleneck. A full-speed version should use
  fixslice/bitslice or carefully evaluated BMI2 pext/pdep.
