Notes:
  TWINE is implemented in a separate file. Segment timing shows neither the
  scalar S-box layer nor the scalar permutation layer alone dominates; the cost
  comes from repeating the round 36 times and moving data between scalar block
  layout and vector nibble-slice layout. The optimized path packs 16 blocks by
  nibble position into ymm registers. The S-box maps to vpshufb through
  _mm256_shuffle_epi8; the nibble permutation maps to register reassignment.
